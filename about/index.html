Meet the founder

Dr. Benjamin Obire, MD

Visionary founder, Health-Hub Africa

Dr. Benjamin Obire, MD is the visionary founder of Health-Hub Africa, a pioneering healthcare organization dedicated to transforming healthcare delivery in Nigeria.

Dr. Obire’s remarkable career began humbly as a help desk analyst in the United States, where his relentless drive for growth propelled him to hold critical roles, including Solutions Architect, Chief Information Officer (CIO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), and eventually, business owner. Over the course of three decades in the U.S., he acquired extensive expertise across multiple industries, specializing in cybersecurity, IT solutions, and federal operations.

Dr. Obire worked extensively in the information technology space, advancing from system engineering roles in healthcare, telecommunications, and cybersecurity to solution architecture positions in education, manufacturing, finance, state government, and the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD). His work focused on securing sensitive data, ensuring regulatory compliance, and designing robust, resilient infrastructures.

Driven by a passion to make a greater impact, Dr. Obire made a bold transition from the technology sector to the medical field. Inspired by a deep commitment to improving patient care, he earned his Doctor of Medicine degree and moved into clinical practice. This rare combination of technical prowess and medical expertise positions Dr. Obire as a trailblazer in healthcare innovation, seamlessly integrating cutting-edge technology with patient-centered care to address critical healthcare challenges.

This unique career evolution led him to establish Health-Hub Africa, where he applies his diverse skill set to deliver innovative, accessible, and high-quality healthcare solutions in Nigeria. The organization’s offerings—ranging from emergency response units and telemedicine to diagnostic services and 24/7 minute clinics—are built on patent-pending, copyrighted, and trademarked solutions, solidifying Health-Hub Africa’s leadership in tech-enabled healthcare.

Prior to founding Health-Hub Africa, Dr. Obire held influential roles as Chief Operating Officer (COO) and Chief Technology Officer (CTO) in the U.S. He also successfully founded and sold an IT company, further showcasing his entrepreneurial acumen. His cybersecurity expertise remains a cornerstone of his work, ensuring patient data is safeguarded in an increasingly digital healthcare landscape.

Dr. Obire’s journey—from a U.S.-based help desk analyst to a pioneering healthcare leader—embodies resilience, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to change. By merging his deep experience in IT and cybersecurity with his medical training, he is redefining the future of healthcare in Nigeria, creating a system that is secure, accessible, and compassionate for all.

Technology matters most when it helps care arrive faster, travel further and feel more human.

Our work is guided by four principles: responsive service, clinical quality, secure systems and compassionate delivery.
