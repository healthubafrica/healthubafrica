Meet the founder

Dr. Benjamin Obire, MD

Visionary founder, Health-Hub Africa

Dr. Benjamin Obire, MD is the visionary founder of Health-Hub Africa, a pioneering healthcare organization dedicated to transforming healthcare delivery in Nigeria.

Over three decades, Dr. Obire worked extensively across cybersecurity, IT solutions, and federal operations in the United States, advancing from systems engineering to CIO, CTO, and business owner. He designed resilient infrastructures for healthcare, telecommunications, state government, and the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD).

Driven by a commitment to improve patient care, Dr. Obire earned his Doctor of Medicine degree and entered clinical practice. This rare combination of technological acumen and medical training led him to establish Health-Hub Africa—delivering tech-enabled, patent-pending healthcare models built for African realities.
