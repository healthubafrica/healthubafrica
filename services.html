01 / EMERGENCY

DispatchCare™

Our patented Emergency Medical Dispatch Response Units deploy trained teams with emergency-grade equipment, bypassing traffic to deliver immediate care at the point of need.

  • Rapid motorcycle response units
  • On-scene assessment and stabilization
  • Hospital coordination when further treatment is needed
  • Support for homes, workplaces, communities and events

02 / WALK-IN

MinuteCare™

Our 24/7 walk-in clinics provide convenient, high-quality care for minor illnesses, preventive health, routine checkups and vaccinations without long waits.

  • Everyday illness and minor injury care
  • Routine checks and preventive services
  • Vaccinations and immunizations
  • Women’s health and routine children’s care
03 / DIAGNOSTICS

CareTest™

We provide fast, reliable diagnostic testing to support early detection and effective treatment, with a focus on accurate and timely results.

  • Routine and preventive health screenings
  • Blood, urine and infectious-disease testing
  • Women’s health diagnostics and medical imaging
  • Clear results with professional guidance
04 / VIRTUAL

TeleCare™

Our 24/7 virtual consultations connect patients with healthcare professionals from home or work, making quality medical support more accessible.

  • General and follow-up consultations
  • Chronic-condition and prescription support
  • Mental health and specialist access
  • Pediatric, preventive and lifestyle guidance
05 / TRAVEL

TravelSafe™

TravelSafe™ combines pre-travel preparation, real-time medical support, on-ground emergency response and post-event continuity of care within the Health-Hub Africa ecosystem.

  • Pre-travel health assessment and readiness planning
  • 24/7 medical guidance through TeleCare™
  • Emergency coordination through DispatchCare™
  • Secure records through MyHealth Vault+™
  • Post-travel and post-event follow-up
06 / RECORDS

MyHealth Vault+™

Health-Hub Africa’s secure, AI-powered digital health record platform transforms fragmented medical data into a single, living profile for prevention, care coordination, emergency response and long-term wellness.

  • Patient-owned unified health record
  • Emergency-ready health profile
  • AI-assisted preventive health insights
  • Permission-based sharing and access history
  • Family and dependent health management
07 / ADVISORY

HealthConsult™

Our strategic consulting and personalized medicine service combines physician-led insight with deep systems and technology expertise to improve health decisions, strengthen systems and deliver safer care.

  • Personalized medicine and preventive health
  • Healthcare systems and operations consulting
  • Digital health and information-systems advisory
  • Corporate and workforce health strategy
  • Public health, policy and programme advisory

Conditions and care

Support across more health needs.

Our emergency, clinic, virtual and diagnostic services support urgent needs, everyday illness, long-term care and prevention.

Emergency care

Heart attacks and chest pain, stroke, severe allergic reactions, trauma, breathing difficulties, seizures, severe bleeding and other critical conditions.

Common illnesses

Colds, flu, ear and sinus infections, allergies, fever, headaches, skin conditions, minor cuts and burns, and gastrointestinal concerns.

Chronic conditions

Ongoing support for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, COPD, heart disease, arthritis, thyroid disorders and chronic kidney disease.

Women & children

Women’s health, reproductive and cervical care, pediatric illness support, vaccinations, school physicals and wellness examinations.

Prevention & diagnostics

Annual checkups, vaccinations, health screenings, blood tests, infectious-disease testing, medical imaging and cancer screening.

Mental health

Support for depression, anxiety, stress, trauma, grief, relationship concerns and sleep disorders through TeleCare™.
